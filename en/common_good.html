Social Influence

Aiming to be the best DRAM partner for the smart world, Nanya Technology strives to drive revenue and return profits to its shareholders. At the same time, Nanya Technology actively engages in public welfare activities, creates positive impact, and moves toward mutual prosperity with its stakeholders. The Company aligns its core value with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and has developed four social engagement pillars unique to Nanya Technology, including Talent Cultivation, Environmental Conservation, Humanistic Care, and Community Harmony. Based on these four pillars, we design a range of action plans with short, medium, and long term goals. By integrating Nanya Technology's internal and external resources, we collaborate with stakeholders such as customers, suppliers, government agencies, schools, and NGOs to create greater impact through social engagement actions.

  • Talent Cultivation
    Sustainable Education Blueprint – Instilling ESG Sustainability Concepts in Youth

    Nanya Technology is committed to tackling various sustainability issues and actively promotes awareness and understanding of Environment, Social, Governance (ESG), Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), climate change, and more. We combined our resources with school to provide in-depth ESG training to each student, and enable sustainability concepts to flourish on campus. The sustainability education blueprint aims to foster connections across industry, government, academia, and research. Beyond cultivating technological talents, we further support the development of diverse skill sets and promote interdisciplinary excellence among students to achieve quality education.

    • Educated 1,387 students in total
    • Sports: table tennis, taekwondo, and competitive gymnastics, basketball

      Sponsored 2 national athletes and 5 sports talents

    • Design Thinking ・ Creative Integration

      For three consecutive years, Nanya Technology has collaborated with Ming Chi University of Technology, integrating the school's University Social Responsibility (USR) initiatives with design thinking courses to instill a deep understanding of sustainability in students' hearts.

    • Student-Athlete Scholarship

      Nanya Technology's education blueprint extends beyond graduate schools, colleges, and high schools in the DRAM industry. Starting in 2022, Nanya Technology began providing the Student-Athlete Scholarship to colleagues' children, serves as a resource provider, offering student athletes with stable financial support during their training. Cumulative sponsorship has reached NT$650,000 as of 2025.

  • Environmental Conservation
    Earth Hour 60 Earth Day 365: Responding to International Environmental Conservation Actions to Broaden Our Impact

    Nanya Technology organizes a series of Earth Hour 60 Earth Day 365 events to raise awareness about environmental conservation through hands-on experiences. Externally, Nanya Technology works with various partners to support world environmental conservation days; internally, we raise environmental awareness each year with a different theme, thereby increasing everyone's understanding about topics ranging from ecology, natural habitats, to climate change. Through company-organized hands-on activities, Nanya Technology fosters a sense of mission among colleagues to protect the Earth.

    • Network Influence 2,946 Facebook Interactions
    • Arbor Day

      In response to Arbor Day and to promote ESG principles, Nanya Technology collaborated with the Guishan District Office, corporate groups, and local citizens to plant native Taoyuan tree species, the Chinese Fringetree, symbolizing green sustainability. This event announces to publish the Tree Planting and Mainte nance Guidelines, which is the first guidelines in Taiwan setting a new benchmark for sustainable urban development.

    • Toy Recycling Initiative

      Nanya Technology actively promotes circular resource education. On April 12, we hosted a heartwarming Toy Recycling Family Day. Through hands-on recycling and reuse practices, employees and their children learned how to reduce waste by refurbishing old toys and giving them new value. The event not only strengthened family bonds but also planted the seeds of sustainability in the next generation.

    • Wugu Wetland Restoration - Protecting Nature's Filtration System

      Nanya Technology continues to focus on watershed ecology and biodiversity conservation. Since 2020, we have partnered with The Society of Wilderness to mobilize volunteers and their families to remove the invasive species, mikania vine. This fast-growing vine can quickly overtake native vegetation, posing a serious threat to wetlands. It must be removed manually, plant by plant. A total of 155.4 kg has been removed. Volunteers also participated in clearing reeds, reducing competition for habitat and allowing native plants to thrive. This year, a new initiative was introduced to restore leather fern, a species which serves as a critical ecological indicator. The combination of adding restoration efforts (leather fern) and reducing invasive species (mikania vine) reflects a commitment to ecological resilience. Through sustained volunteer engagement, these actions contribute to the long-term health and biodiversity of the Wugu Wetland.

  • Humanistic Care
    Promoting Cultural Industries – Salvaging Local Crafts and Creating New Memories

    Partners: Ministry of Culture's Taiwan Creative Content Agency, Taishan District Office, Ming Chi University of Technology, Upper Taishan Temple, Mingzhi Academy, EZ Show Drum Studio, Tien Circus Theatre, Corny Chicken Theatre, Professor Jean May Tsiang Culture and Education Foundation.

    • Enhancing domestic cultural industries with annual, funding of NT$ 3 Million
    • Supporting Domestic Cultural Industry Development

      Nanya Technology actively contributes resources to domestic film and arts groups. Through family days, movie screenings, and parent-child activities, we invite arts groups to perform, not only driving diverse cultural and artistic development but also encouraging employees to engage with cultural activities and cultivate an appreciation for the arts. By sponsoring arts and cultural events, Nanya Technology builds partnerships that create win-win situations for both the economy and culture, contributing to a more diverse and vibrant cultural landscape in Taiwan.

    • Community Building Program: Integrating Local Resources to Characterize Regional Humanistic Landscape

      In partnership with Ming Chi University of Technology and the Ministry of Education's USR initiative, specifically the Sprout Project of Northern Taiwan First Academy Cultural Corridor Development Program, we jointly promote the Taishan Renaissance activities. Through collaboration with local arts and cultural groups, we promote cultural education through guided tours and a variety of hands-on activities. These efforts support community development, helping the public understand local temple traditions and unique cultural characteristics, while creating new experiences from traditional craftsmanship.

    • Taishan Lion Dance Cultural Festival

      The New Taipei City Taishan Lion Dance Cultural Festival, an important cultural event in the Taishan area, is hosted annually by the Taishan District Office with Nanya Technology serving as a co-organizer. This event began as a celebration of the birthday of "Master Xianying," the main deity of both Upper Taishan Temple and Lower Taishan Temple (celebrated annually on September 18 in the lunar calendar). Due to the frequent inclusion of lion dance performances in temple celebrations, the Taishan Lion Dance Championship was created to showcase this cultural tradition. As the ceremonial activities grew larger, what started as a local cultural event has since 2006 evolved into Taiwan's landmark lion dance competition, the Taishan Lion King Championship, which has been held for 19 consecutive years. Nanya Technology has sponsored this traditional local event for 8 consecutive years.

  • Community Harmony
    Partnering with Neighborhoods: Engaging in Neighborhood Public Affairs to Create a Harmonious and Mutually Beneficial Living Environment
    • Sponsored a total of approximately NT$ 1.3 million
    • Year-End Dining Gathering

      Understanding the needs of local disadvantaged groups, Nanya Technology has been regularly collaborating with Nan Ya Plastics Corporation and the Taishan District Office since 2020 to organize year-end dining gatherings. These events aim to address local needs and provide care for disadvantaged residents in the community.

    • Protecting Road Users' Safety

      Located in the Nanlin Science Park in Taishan District, Nanya Technology is connected by an access road that serves as a key route for both residents and employees. The road is narrow and poses higher traffic risks during peak hours, further compounded by ongoing plant expansion. To enhance road safety, we supported the Taishan District Office through a phased road improvement program launched in 2022. Completed in 2025, the road upgrades included drainage coverage, channel desilting and widening of selected road segments.

    • Promotion of Local Arts

      Nanya Technology supports arts and cultural promotion and deeply values local communities. In collaboration with Ming Chi University of Technology, we promote local cultural and artistic activities in Taishan, enriching residents' and children's artistic exposure and fostering community engagement.

Love Connection

To expand our impact and bring various charitable causes to light, Nanya Technology has started Love Connection in 2021. This program encourages employees to propose initiatives based on social issues they care about. Nanya Technology provides funding to support employees and turn these meaningful ideas into real actions, spreading kindness throughout the Company and fostering a positive cycle. In 2025, a total of seven initiatives concluded successfully. These programs not only advanced the original goals of social engagement but also provided a platform for employees to participate, exchange insights, and work together toward building a better society. We will continue to advance this program, bringing together more people and resources to create a greater positive impact on society.

Nanya Technology Volunteer Team

To enhance Nanya Technology's commitment to social engagement, the Nanya Technology Volunteer Team was established in 2021. The President serves as the Convener, the Executive Vice President as Deputy Convener, and the Vice President as Team Leader to strengthen volunteer recruitment efforts. Employee volunteers are the driving force behind the company's commitment to social responsibility. Although participation was limited at the team's establishment in 2021 due to the pandemic, numbers grew after restrictions lifted in 2022. By 2025, a total of 240 dedicated employees had joined the volunteer team, steadily strengthening its impact. Over the past year, employee volunteers participated in 16 physical social engagement activities and were actively involved in a wide range of initiatives, including holiday charity bazaars, Arbor Day events, charity runs, local arts and cultural programs, and environmental conservation activities. We encourage and appreciate our employee volunteers for their commitment to social engagement and their willingness to give back to society in diverse ways despite their busy work schedules. Through structured coordination and clear task delegation, volunteers not only serve as a strong force in advancing social and sustainability initiatives, but also deepen their understanding of social engagement issues through hands on participation. Looking ahead, we will continue to encourage more employees to join the volunteer team, harness collective strength, expand positive social impact, and advance our goal of shared value for both the company and society.
